A one-page front page summing up the world's news of Monday, 23 November 2026: Ukraine's sea drones sink a Russian warship in Benghazi, COP31 ends with no deal, tankers are hit in the Strait of Hormuz, Russian missiles enter Poland, chip stocks slide on AI doubts, and Democrats take the US House.

`main.py` copies `news.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is 1024x1024 and deterministic — two runs write identical bytes.

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123).
